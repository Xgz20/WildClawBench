Appendix 3: Phase field model

In a phase field model [ 29 ], one decomposes strain \({\varvec{\varepsilon }}\) according to its eigenvectors \({\varvec{n}}^1,{\varvec{n}}^2,{\varvec{n}}^3\) , namely,

$$\begin{aligned} {\varvec{\varepsilon }}= {\varvec{\varepsilon }}_++{\varvec{\varepsilon }}_-,\quad {\varvec{\varepsilon }}_\pm =\sum _{a=1}^3\langle \varepsilon ^a\rangle _\pm {\varvec{M}}_a, \quad {\varvec{M}}_a {:=}{\varvec{n}}^a\otimes {\varvec{n}}^a, \end{aligned}$$ (35)

where \(\varepsilon ^a\) is the corresponding eigenvalue, and \(\langle \varepsilon ^a\rangle _\pm {:=}\frac{1}{2}(\varepsilon ^a\pm|\varepsilon ^a|)\) .

Next, we form a fully isotropic constitutive assumption for the degradation of strain energy

$$\begin{aligned} \psi ({\varvec{\varepsilon }},d) &= (1-d)^2 \psi _0^+ + \psi _0^-, \\ \psi _0^{\pm }({\varvec{\varepsilon }})&{:=} \frac{\lambda }{2}\langle \text{tr}({\varvec{\varepsilon }})\rangle _{\pm }^2+\mu \text{tr}({\varvec{\varepsilon }}_{\pm }^2). \end{aligned}$$ (36)

Here \(\lambda \) and \(\mu \) are Lamé coefficients of the linear homogeneous elastic material.

Now invoking time as an additional parameter, we define the maximal positive strain energy in history, namely, the history field

$$\begin{aligned} \mathcal {H} ({\varvec{\varepsilon }},t){:=}\max _{s\in [0,t]}\psi _0^+({\varvec{\varepsilon }}({\varvec{x}};s)). \end{aligned}$$ (37)

For an elastic material, the constitutive relation gives stress from strain by

$$\begin{aligned} {\varvec{\sigma }}({\varvec{x}},d,{\varvec{\varepsilon }}){:=}\frac{\partial \psi }{\partial {\varvec{\varepsilon }}}. \end{aligned}$$ (38)

This may be put into a standard form of \({\varvec{\sigma }}({\varvec{x}},d,{\varvec{\varepsilon }})={\varvec{C}}({\varvec{x}},d,{\varvec{\varepsilon }}):{\varvec{\varepsilon }}({\varvec{x}})\) . Here the damaged tangent stiffness \({\varvec{C}}({\varvec{x}},d,{\varvec{\varepsilon }})\) is also the damaged secant stiffness, which explicitly reads

$$\begin{aligned} {\varvec{C}}&{:=}\frac{\partial {\varvec{\sigma }}}{\partial {\varvec{\varepsilon }}}=(1-d)^2 \left[ 3\lambda \tilde{H}(\text{tr}({\varvec{\varepsilon }}))\mathbb {I}^{\text{vol}} + 2\mu \mathbb {P}^+\right] \\ & \quad + \left[ 3\lambda \tilde{H}(-\text{tr}({\varvec{\varepsilon }}))\mathbb {I}^{\text{vol}} +2\mu \mathbb {P}^-\right] \end{aligned}$$ (39)

$$\begin{aligned} \mathbb {P}^{\pm }&:= \frac{\partial {\varvec{\varepsilon }}^{\pm }}{\partial {\varvec{\varepsilon }}} \\& = \sum _a^3 \tilde{H}(\pm \varepsilon _a) ({\varvec{M}}_a)_{ij} ({\varvec{M}}_{a})_{kl} \\ & \quad + \frac{1}{2} \sum _a^3 \sum _{b\ne a}^3 \theta _{ab} [({\varvec{M}}_a)_{ik} ({\varvec{M}}_b)_{jl} \\ & \quad + ({\varvec{M}}_b)_{il} ({\varvec{M}}_b)_{jk}] \end{aligned}$$ (40)

Here \(\tilde{H}(\cdot )\) is the Heaviside function and \(\theta _{ab}\) is defined as

$$\begin{aligned} \theta _{ab}=\left\{ \begin{aligned}&\frac{\langle \varepsilon _a \rangle _{\pm } -\langle \varepsilon _b \rangle _{\pm } }{\varepsilon _a -\varepsilon _b},&\text{if} \; \varepsilon _a \ne \varepsilon _b, \\&\tilde{H}(\pm \varepsilon _a),&\text{if} \; \varepsilon _a =\varepsilon _b. \end{aligned} \right. \end{aligned}$$ (41)

The governing equation and boundary condition are

$$\begin{aligned} \left\{ \begin{aligned}&\frac{g_c}{l} \left( d - l^2 \Delta d\right) = 2(1-d)\mathcal {H}, \quad {\varvec{x}}\in \Omega, \\&(\nabla d) \cdot {\varvec{n}} = 0, \quad {\varvec{x}}\in \partial \Omega, \end{aligned} \right. \end{aligned}$$ (42)
