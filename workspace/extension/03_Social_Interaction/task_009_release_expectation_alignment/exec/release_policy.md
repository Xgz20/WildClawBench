# Release commitment policy

- Working targets and conditional internal windows are not customer commitments.
- Do not commit a production date until security review, four-times load testing, rollback rehearsal, and written Release Manager approval are complete.
- When a customer has interpreted a target as guaranteed, acknowledge the prior expectation, correct the commitment status, and provide a specific next-update time.
- Do not blame Sales, Engineering, or the customer in external or internal drafts.
