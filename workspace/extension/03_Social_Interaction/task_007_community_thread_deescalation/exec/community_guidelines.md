# Community moderation rules

- `R1 Personal attacks`: remove direct insults about another participant. A first violation receives a friendly reminder. A repeated R1 violation within 30 days receives a formal warning.
- Keep disagreement about ideas. Posts that state a position without attacking a participant remain visible.
- If at least five unhandled flags arrive within 30 minutes, temporarily close the thread for four hours.
- Reopen only after flagged posts are reviewed, required content actions are complete, flags are cleared, and a staff notice asks participants to return to the topic.
- Public notices must not identify reporters or disclose private moderation history. All outputs are drafts; moderators perform any real action after review.
