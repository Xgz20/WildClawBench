# Delay response and approval policy

- Incremental contingency spend up to CNY 15,000 is within the emergency budget but requires CFO approval when any selected option lists CFO.
- Reserved support inventory cannot be reassigned to launch work.
- A shipping date or shipping window is not an arrival commitment. Preserve the supplier's qualifiers.
- Hardware becomes launch-available only after arrival and the full two-calendar-day QA period.
- The pilot may be reduced to 120 units with Program Director approval.
- Regional launch may remain conditional when at least 800 QA-cleared units are available by October 16 and all listed approvals are complete.
- General availability cannot be committed until all 1,200 units have arrived, passed QA, and the Release Manager has approved the final gate.
- Escalate if the first batch misses its October 10 ship date, estimated October 13 arrival, or QA completion by October 15; also escalate if the remaining batch has no committed arrival by October 16.
- Drafts must not place orders, approve spend, send messages, or describe an unapproved date as committed.
