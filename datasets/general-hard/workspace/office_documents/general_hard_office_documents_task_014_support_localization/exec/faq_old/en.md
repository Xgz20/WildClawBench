# NoriDesk 旧FAQ en

旧资料日期2026-09-20。仅供修订对照，当前事实以release_notes.md为准。

## K01 Which release should I use?

The stable version is 3.1.0. Version 3.2.1 will be generally available on every platform by 2026-10-02.

## K02 What are the upload limits?

Each file may be 25 MB. A batch can contain ten files with no aggregate size cap.

## K03 What can a CSV export contain?

Every plan and role can export 10,000 rows with all attachments included.

## K04 Are scanned PDFs supported?

OCR is already available for all scanned and text PDFs and can be enabled immediately.

## K05 How do I resolve duplicate sync records?

Install the stable Windows 3.2.1 patch; it has permanently fixed the issue.

## K06 Can I work offline?

Cached content is accessible for 24 hours after authentication, then it is deleted. CSV export remains available offline.

## K07 May a Viewer export data?

A Viewer may export if the plan has enough rows. Support can grant the role change in its reply.

## K08 Can we use SSO in eu-fr?

EU preview needs Enterprise, Owner, macOS, version 3.2.1 and enrollment. It is guaranteed to become generally available by 2026-10-10.

## K09 How long are audit records retained?

Every plan retains records for 90 days, with guaranteed backup recovery afterward.

## K10 Which orders qualify for a refund?

All monthly, annual and reseller orders are entitled to an immediate full refund within 30 days.

## K11 Does SSO preview enable migration?

EU SSO enrollment automatically authorizes migration with workspace IDs and attachments preserved.

## K12 What is the support SLA?

Support runs 24/7 and guarantees both a first reply and full resolution within two calendar days.
